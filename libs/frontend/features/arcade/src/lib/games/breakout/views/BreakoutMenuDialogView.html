Move the active paddle. A and D also work. Serve a ball. In Catch games, hold to catch and release to launch.
Move and serve with the right partner paddle. Pause or resume. Escape also works.

Move the mouse to place a paddle directly. Click to serve; hold and release to catch and relaunch. With partners, the pointer controls the paddle in its half of the field.

Each team has five balls and two walls to clear. Each wall has six rows of eighteen bricks. From the bottom up, the rows score 1, 1, 4, 4, 7 and 7 points per brick: 432 per wall, 864 in total.

The part of the paddle you hit changes the rebound angle. The angle also changes as the rally continues. The ball speeds up after twelve paddle hits or when it strikes a brick in the top three rows.

Variations 1–4 are Breakout, Steerable, Catch and Invisible. Variations 5–8 add a cumulative timer to the same four games. Variations 9–12 are their Breakthru versions: the ball travels through bricks instead of bouncing off them.

In a timed game, the clock counts up during play, including a held catch; there is no time limit. Clear both walls as quickly as possible. Untimed games reward the highest score; at the maximum score, use the fewest balls.

One player plays alone. Two players oppose each other. Three players form a pair against one; four players form two pairs. Opposing teams alternate after losing a ball. Partners play together, with one paddle in each half of the field.

Difficulty A gives a wide paddle. Difficulty B reduces its width to three quarters. Choose the two teams' difficulty switches separately.
