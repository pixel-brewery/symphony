Player 1: move the left paddle up and down.
Player 2: move the right paddle up and down. Pause or resume. Escape also works.

Move the pointer up or down in either half of the court to position that player's paddle directly. The keyboard and pointer replace the cabinet's two rotary knobs.

Return the ball with your paddle. Miss it and your opponent scores. The first player to reach the selected 11 or 15 points wins; no two-point lead is required.

The part of the paddle the ball touches changes its angle. Central contacts can send it straight across; edges produce steeper rebounds. The ball speeds up after four and twelve paddle hits, and slows down again after a point.

There is a short automatic serve delay after each point. The next ball travels toward the player who missed. A small gap above each paddle is part of the original cabinet's behavior.

Menus, pause, sound controls and saved match results are Symphony additions. There is no computer opponent in this original two-player version.
