# Review German and EU compliance

1. Define the exact technical scope, data flows, actors, storage, retention, automation, and jurisdictions.
2. Use current official primary legal and regulator sources; browse because legal guidance changes.
3. Separate clear technical defects from missing business facts and counsel-only decisions.
4. Review data minimization, purpose limitation, access, deletion, auditability, credential protection, transparency, and user control as applicable.
5. Correct only authorized task-scoped technical defects.
6. Never claim complete legal compliance. Report findings, evidence, residual uncertainty, and required professional review as appropriate.
